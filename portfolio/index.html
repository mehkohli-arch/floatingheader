    About me

    I'm a UX/UI designer at CMG Financial, where I'm the company-wide Subject Matter Expert for design systems. I like the hard parts of enterprise software: dense data, regulated workflows, and many teams shipping into the same product.

    I design features end to end and partner closely with engineering on the component library, PrimeNG performance and accessibility compliance. My work has made employee tasks 18–25% faster, cut user errors by 12% and raised feature adoption by 15%.
