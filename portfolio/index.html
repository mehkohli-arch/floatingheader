    About me

    I'm a senior UX/UI designer at CMG Financial, a US mortgage lender, and the company's go-to expert on design systems. I love taking complicated software (lots of data, strict rules, many teams) and making it feel simple for the people who use it every day.

    I lead products from first research to launch, and work closely with engineers so what ships is fast and accessible to everyone, including people who use screen readers or keyboards. My work has made everyday tasks 18–25% faster for employees, cut user errors by 12% and increased use of new features by 15%.

    I'm an AI-enabled designer. I use AI throughout my work, from building design systems to prototyping, and I also design AI-powered products.

    I came to design from psychology, and understanding how people think still shapes how I design.
